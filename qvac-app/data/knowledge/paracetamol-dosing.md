# Paracetamol (acetaminophen) — dosing limits and overdose

> DEMO CORPUS — illustrative content for retrieval evaluation; not a clinical reference.

## Maximum doses
- Adults ≥50 kg: 1 g per dose, max **4 g/24 h**, minimum 4 h between doses.
- Adults <50 kg, hepatic impairment, chronic alcohol use, or cachexia: max **3 g/24 h**
  (some protocols 2 g).
- Children: 15 mg/kg per dose, max 4 doses/24 h.

## Hidden paracetamol
The classic overdose route is stacking: branded cold-and-flu products, co-codamol, and
plain paracetamol taken together. Always total ALL sources when a patient reports "it's
not working so I took extra."

## Overdose thresholds
A single ingestion ≥**75 mg/kg** is potentially toxic; ≥150 mg/kg is serious. Toxicity is
initially **asymptomatic** — absence of symptoms in the first 24 h is NOT reassuring.
Hepatotoxicity manifests at 24–72 h (RUQ pain, vomiting, jaundice).

## Action
Any ingestion over threshold, staggered overdose, or uncertain total within the last 24 h:
refer to ED immediately — N-acetylcysteine is near-fully protective when started within
**8 hours** of ingestion. Never advise "wait and see how you feel."
