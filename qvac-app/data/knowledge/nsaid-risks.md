# NSAID sale screening — who should not get ibuprofen

> DEMO CORPUS — illustrative content for retrieval evaluation; not a clinical reference.

## Contraindications / cautions at the counter
- **Asthma**: ~10% of asthmatics have aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease; any history
  of NSAID-triggered wheeze is an absolute contraindication. Offer paracetamol.
- **Pregnancy**: avoid throughout; **contraindicated in the third trimester** (premature
  closure of ductus arteriosus, oligohydramnios).
- **Peptic ulcer / GI bleed history**: avoid OTC; combined with age >65 or anticoagulants
  the bleeding risk multiplies.
- **Renal impairment, heart failure, cirrhosis**: NSAIDs reduce renal perfusion
  (prostaglandin-mediated); risk of acute kidney injury — especially with the
  "**triple whammy**": ACE inhibitor + diuretic + NSAID.
- **Anticoagulants** (warfarin, DOACs): see warfarin monograph — bleeding risk.
- **Lithium and methotrexate**: NSAIDs reduce renal clearance of both — toxicity.

## Counselling on sale
Lowest effective dose, with food, max 3 days for pain/fever self-care before review.
Ibuprofen OTC ceiling 1.2 g/day (prescription max 2.4 g/day).

## Red flags during NSAID use
Black stools or coffee-ground vomit (GI bleed) — emergency referral. New ankle swelling,
reduced urine output — same-day review.
