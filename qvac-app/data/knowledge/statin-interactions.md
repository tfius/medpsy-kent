# Statins — interaction-driven myopathy

> DEMO CORPUS — illustrative content for retrieval evaluation; not a clinical reference.

## CYP3A4 inhibitors (simvastatin, atorvastatin)
- **Clarithromycin / erythromycin**: marked rise in statin exposure; standard practice is
  to **pause simvastatin or atorvastatin for the antibiotic course**. A patient collecting
  clarithromycin while on simvastatin 40 mg is a classic intervention.
- **Azole antifungals** (itraconazole, ketoconazole): same mechanism, same action.
- **Grapefruit juice**: meaningful with simvastatin (avoid) — modest with atorvastatin,
  negligible with rosuvastatin/pravastatin (not CYP3A4-cleared; useful switch options).

## Other interactions
Ciclosporin, gemfibrozil (avoid with statins), amiodarone and amlodipine (cap simvastatin
at 20 mg), fusidic acid (stop statin during and for 7 days after).

## Recognition — myopathy to rhabdomyolysis
New diffuse muscle pain, tenderness or weakness (esp. proximal: thighs, shoulders) on a
statin warrants a CK check. **Dark/cola-coloured urine** with muscle pain suggests
rhabdomyolysis — emergency referral (risk of acute kidney injury).

Distinguish from common, benign statin aches: mild, symmetrical, no weakness, normal CK —
manage by dose review/switch, not emergency.
